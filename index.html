FAQ

Before you reach out

How long does a typical project take? +
Simple websites: 1–2 weeks. Web apps and automation builds: 3–6 weeks depending on scope, confirmed on the discovery call.
Do you work with businesses outside your city? +
Yes — calls, design reviews and delivery all happen remotely over video call, WhatsApp and email.
I don't have a design ready — do I need one? +
No. Most clients start with a rough idea. The discovery call turns that into a clear spec before any code is written.
Who owns the code after the project? +
You do. Full source code and credentials are handed over once the final payment clears.
What if something breaks after launch? +
Growth and Custom projects include a support window after launch. After that, fixes continue on a small monthly retainer or per-fix basis.
How does payment work? +
Typically 50% upfront to start, 50% on delivery. Larger projects can be split into milestones.
